Controls:
Arrow Keys or WASD to move
"Esc" or "P" to pull up and close the pause menu
Spacebar to freeze time around you
"R" to respawn at the most recent checkpoint with full health and balanced temperature

Things to Note:
- There is music, so listen to it.
- Losing all health respawns you automatically
- You can choose your spawn location by standing on a spawn pad
- freezing time slowly freezes you (temp)
